### 👋 Hey there

I'm a mechanical engineer who fell for AI and never looked back. These days I build things where **AI meets real life**: a device you just *talk to*, models that run on the edge, and pipelines that read documents so humans don't have to.

- 🏥 **Building** [**Cosight**](https://cosight.net/): a voice-first AI device that keeps patients out of the hospital. You talk, and the agent handles reminders, vitals, emergency calls and doctor updates.
- 🎓 **Teaching** practical Python & ML at [**SchoolWhool**](https://www.schoolwhool.com/), with animated explainers I code in Manim.
- 🧪 **Tinkering** with AI agents, voice AI and small models on small devices.
- 🚀 **Been there:** Trained image gen model ann built Cloth2Life, a GenAI try-on SaaS · [published](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12524-023-01703-2) deep learning research with Springer
- 💬 **Ask me about** Edge AI, LLM pipelines, diffusion models, health tech, or going from mechanical engineering to AI.
